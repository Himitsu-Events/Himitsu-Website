About us

Who
We Are

Himitsu is a comunity of event professionals who have spent years working at the sharp end of live events. The three founders collectively have over three decades of experience in the events industry. With a shared passion and severe love for live music, the spectacular production behind it and an obsession with the technology - Himitsu was born out of us believeing that the equipment is just as important to the art form as the music itself!

We specialsise in using Void Acousitcs systems as there is simply nothing else like them - Crafted in the UK, sonically and aesthetically they are phenomenal. And we want to share that with as many people as possible!
